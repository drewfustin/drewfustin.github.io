The program

Socorro household texts are sent by Drew Fustin through Socorro, the Fustin family's private household assistant, from her own phone number. Messages include calendar reminders, schedule changes, answers to questions the recipient asked, and pictures the recipient requested. There is no marketing.

Who gets texts, and how to sign up

Texts go only to members of the Fustin household. Each person agrees in person to get texts from Socorro, then opts in by texting Socorro's number first. Socorro's first reply confirms the sign-up:

Message frequency and cost

Stopping and getting help

  • Reply STOP at any time to stop getting texts. You'll get one message confirming you're unsubscribed, and no more after that.
