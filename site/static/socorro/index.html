Socorro

Socorro is the Fustin family's private household assistant. She runs on Drew Fustin's home computer in Chicago and helps the members of our household with calendars, reminders, email, and questions.

She also sends and receives text messages from her own phone number, only with members of the Fustin household who have agreed to get them. She is not a public service and does not send marketing.
